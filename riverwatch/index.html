Status logic

NORMAL / WATCH

Use official station thresholds where available. WATCH can also be used for a verified rising condition while below an official warning threshold.

ALARM / CRITICAL

These labels should be driven by official thresholds or an authorized government advisory—not by an invented app threshold.

Rate of rise

When two timed readings exist, RiverWatch calculates meters/hour and displays rising, falling or stable trend. This is situational awareness, not a flood forecast.

Station inventory source: Provincial Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office, Provincial Government of Bulacan. Intended live-data source: DOST-ASTI PhilSensors / authorized government feeds. This MVP is for situational awareness and testing; it does not declare structural bridge safety and must not replace official warnings or field assessment.
